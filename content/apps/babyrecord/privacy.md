# Privacy Policy for SproutNote: Baby Tracker (iOS)

Last updated: **October 18, 2026**

Effective date: **October 18, 2026**

SproutNote: Baby Tracker (also displayed as SproutNote, 宝宝成长录 or 寶寶成長錄) is provided by **广州洪软科技有限公司 (Guangzhou Hongruan Technology Co., Ltd.)**, referred to in this policy as "we", "us" or "our".

This policy describes the current **iOS version without Umeng analytics or performance-monitoring SDKs**. Android versions have a different SDK configuration and are covered by the privacy policy provided within the Android app.

## 1. Your Baby's Records Stay on Your Device

SproutNote helps parents and caregivers maintain baby care records. The information you choose to enter or add may include:

- Baby profile details, such as a nickname, sex, date of birth and profile photo.
- Feeding, sleep and diaper records, including dates, times, amounts, durations and notes.
- Growth measurements, such as height, weight and head circumference.
- Milestones and growth moments, including titles, notes, photos and videos.
- App preferences, such as your language selection.

The app stores this information in its local database and private file storage to display your records, calculate summaries and charts, and support editing and local sleep timing. Selected or captured photos and videos are copied into the app's private storage so they can remain available as part of your records.

**We do not receive or automatically upload these baby records, photos or videos.** The current iOS app does not provide accounts, social networking, cloud synchronization or a developer-operated backup service. Core recording features work without an internet connection.

The current iOS app does not include Umeng, advertising or other third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not send page-view events, advertising identifiers or device identifiers to us for analytics or advertising. Apple services, such as App Store diagnostics and device backups, are governed by Apple's privacy practices and your device settings.

## 2. Camera, Photos and Microphone Permissions

The app requests access when you choose a feature that needs it:

- **Camera:** to take a baby profile photo or capture photos and videos for growth moments.
- **Photo library:** to select a profile photo or add existing photos and videos to a growth moment. Where iOS grants limited photo access, the app supports using the items you allow.
- **Microphone:** to record sound when you capture a video. It is not required to enter ordinary care records or take a still photo.

When permission has not already been granted, the app explains the purpose before requesting system permission. You can cancel, decline or change access later in iOS Settings. Refusing or withdrawing access prevents the corresponding media action; you can continue using the other local recording features. Changing permissions does not automatically erase media you previously added to the app.

Media access is used to carry out the action you select, not to upload your library or record audio in the background.

## 5. Storage, Retention and Deletion

### Records and media on your device

Your records remain in the app's private storage until removed. You can view and edit records and remove them from the visible lists using the app's controls.

**Removing a record from a list is not an immediate permanent erasure.** The current app marks deleted records as deleted in the local database to support undo. Associated media files, and copies of media replaced in a record or profile, may remain in the app's private storage. The current version has no in-app control to permanently erase all stored data at once.

To remove the app's local database and private media from a device, use **Delete App** in iOS, rather than **Offload App**, which keeps app data. Deleting the app does not delete the original photos or videos in your system photo library. We have no server-side copy of your local records to access, recover or erase remotely.

### System backups and transfers

Depending on your device settings, Apple or your computer may include app data in an iCloud or computer backup, or transfer it when you move to another device. These system features are separate from an in-app cloud-sync service. Deleting the app from a device does not necessarily remove older backup copies. Manage those copies through the relevant Apple or computer backup settings.

We cannot restore your records if the local data is lost and you have no usable backup. Consider this before deleting the app or removing a system backup.

### Security

The app uses iOS private app storage and system permissions to limit access to its files and protected device features. Device security and backups also depend on your iOS settings. Protect your device and backup accounts, and keep iOS up to date. No storage or communication system can guarantee absolute security.
