### 5. Retention and deletion

- **Local data:** Remains on your device while you use the app, without an automatic expiry period. You can view and edit records where the app supports this.
- **Individual record deletion:** Currently uses soft deletion. A record is hidden from normal lists and can be restored through undo, while its database entry and associated media may remain locally. Replacing an avatar or media does not guarantee immediate removal of the old copy. Removing an item from a list is not permanent erasure.
- **Erase local app data:** Go to Android “Settings → Apps → SproutNote / 宝宝成长录 → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage / Clear data.” Labels vary by device. This removes the local app database, settings, and private media and cannot be undone in the app. Uninstalling normally removes private local data too. Manage system backups separately to avoid restoration after reinstalling.
- **Other copies:** Clearing private app data does not erase originals in your gallery, sent emails, separately saved copies, or system backups. Manage these at their respective locations.
- **Support and privacy communications:** Retained only while needed to handle the request, resolve the issue, and complete necessary follow-up. They are deleted or anonymized after that purpose ends unless further retention is legally required. You may request deletion of communications we hold by email.
- **Website technical logs:** Retained for the shortest period necessary for page delivery, troubleshooting, and security, then deleted or anonymized unless further retention is legally required.

We hold no server copy of your baby records and cannot remotely delete, export, or recover them. This version does not offer in-app data export. There are no app accounts to close. You can erase local data through system settings and contact us about messages or other information we have received.
