Where the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we process your personal information on the following legal bases:

- **Performance of a contract** (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): to provide, maintain and improve the PixelBoard App and its features, and to process your requests, complaints and feedback.

- **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): where you have given us consent for a specific purpose, such as participating in social interactions or receiving optional communications. You may withdraw your consent at any time.
