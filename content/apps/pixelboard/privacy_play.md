Versions distributed through other channels or with different features are subject to the policies provided with those versions.

## 1. Overview

Pixel Canvas is a pixel art editor. Drawing, layers, frame animation, artwork management, palettes, reference images and local exports work on your device without registration or sign-in.

- We do not receive or synchronize your artwork, reference images or local settings through an app backend. We do not provide developer-operated artwork cloud storage or a public artwork community.
- The app does not integrate advertising, user behavior analytics or automatic crash-reporting SDKs. We do not use such SDKs to collect device identifiers or build advertising profiles.
- Opening the online privacy policy or user agreement initiates the network requests needed to display those pages.
- If you choose to send feedback by email, we receive the information you decide to send.
- The app allows Android system backup and device transfer. If you enable these services, the system may transmit eligible local app data. This is separate from developer-operated cloud storage.

## 2. Information accessed and stored locally

| Information | Purpose | Storage and transmission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pixel content, layers, frames, artwork names, groups, dimensions, timestamps and related artwork information | Drawing, editing, autosave and artwork management | Stored in app storage on your device; not uploaded to our app backend |
| Images you select through the system selection interface, and reference-image copies generated in the app | Showing references, picking colors and assisting creation | Read and processed locally; not automatically uploaded by the app |
| Palettes, language, canvas or export options, preview positions and agreement acknowledgment settings | Remembering your choices and maintaining the local experience | Stored in app or Android settings; some settings may be included in system backup |
| Exported PNG, GIF, sprite sheet and associated JSON files | Saving the results you request | Saved to your gallery, Downloads or local app directories; not automatically published or sent by the app |
| Necessary temporary files, previews and on-device runtime error records | Displaying artwork, generating exports and troubleshooting local operation | Processed on your device; the app does not automatically send these records to us |

The drawing features do not automatically scan or upload your entire photo library. We do not require your name, phone number, account details or payment information. The drawing features do not request location, contacts, phone, camera or microphone permissions. The app does not collect advertising identifiers or persistent device identifiers through an app backend.

Artwork and images may contain information you draw or choose yourself. The app does not analyze that content to identify people, infer personal characteristics or serve advertising.

If you select an image from a cloud drive or another online file provider, that provider may need a network connection to retrieve it. Your chosen system or file-provider service performs that retrieval under its own data-handling rules.

## 3. Online policy and agreement pages

The app loads an online policy or agreement page only when you choose to open it. Delivering the page requires its server, hosting provider or necessary content-delivery service to receive connection and request information, such as your IP address, request time, requested page and browser or WebView technical information.

Depending on its implementation, a page may use cookies, local storage or a cache for basic website functions. This processing takes place through the page and its providers, separately from local artwork editing. We do not attach your artwork, selected reference images, group names or local creation settings when you open an agreement page. We do not use agreement-page access data for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

We remain responsible for the data handling of agreement pages we control. A third-party hosting platform, such as Yuque, may also process information for its own services under its privacy policy. If we change hosting providers or introduce different data processing, we will update the disclosure and provide any legally required notice or consent before that processing begins.

You can use the local drawing features without opening the online agreement pages. Clearing app storage can remove website data stored within the app, but it also deletes local artwork and settings. Save anything you need before doing so.

## 4. Email feedback

The feedback action opens an email app on your device with our recipient address and a feedback subject prefilled. You decide whether to send a message and what text or attachments to include. Pixel Canvas does not automatically send email or attach artwork, reference images or diagnostic logs.

If you send a message, we may process your sender address, any name or nickname shown in the message, your feedback, and screenshots, attachments or other information you choose to provide. We use this information only to answer inquiries, resolve issues, handle privacy requests and, where necessary, protect related legal rights. We do not use it for advertising or marketing.

Your email app and email providers process transmission and storage under their own service rules. Please provide only what is needed to address the issue, and redact unnecessary personal or sensitive information before sending.

## 5. Android system backup and device transfer

The app permits Android system backup and device transfer. If you enable backup, restore or device migration in your device or system-account settings, the system may transmit eligible artwork files, reference-image copies, settings and other app data to a backup service provided by Google or your device manufacturer, or transfer them to another device.

Whether a backup occurs, what it contains, where it is stored and how long it is retained depend on your Android version, manufacturer, system settings and backup service. The app does not guarantee that every artwork will be backed up or restored. We cannot access, manage or delete your system backups through an app backend.

Manage these features through your device or backup-service settings. Deleting artwork, clearing app data or uninstalling the app may not immediately delete existing backups. Backups may also restore data when you reinstall the app or move to another device.

## 6. Permissions and your choices

- **Internet access:** Used for online policy and agreement pages you choose to open. Your selected email app sends feedback messages; Pixel Canvas does not upload local artwork through its own business APIs.
- **Storage write access:** On Android 9 and earlier, the app may request storage write permission to export artwork to shared storage. You can refuse or revoke this permission in system settings, which may limit the relevant export features.
- **Image selection:** The app accesses images you choose through the system selection interface. It does not request ongoing broad access to your entire photo library for this purpose.

Refusing an optional permission or choosing not to send feedback does not cause your local artwork to be uploaded. Features that do not depend on the permission remain available.

## 7. Disclosures and third-party services

We do not sell or rent personal information or exchange it for targeted advertising. We do not hold backend copies of artwork and settings that remain on your device and have not been sent to us.

In the circumstances described in this policy, relevant information may be handled by:

1. Hosting, network and content-delivery providers needed to display online policy and agreement pages. The currently used pages involve Yuque and our agreement website.
2. The email app and email services you choose, and providers that receive and store email for us.
3. Google, device manufacturers or other system backup and file-provider services you enable or select.
4. Authorities legally entitled to information we actually hold, where required by law or valid legal process. We disclose only information necessary for that purpose.

Google Play, Android and device manufacturers may independently process information for app distribution, system diagnostics or device services under their own policies. This does not mean Pixel Canvas integrates advertising or analytics SDKs.

## 8. Retention and deletion

| Information | Retention period or criteria | How to delete it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local artwork and reference-image copies | Remain on your device so you can save and continue editing, until you delete the relevant data | Delete artwork in the app or clear app storage in Android settings. The system also normally removes app-specific data on uninstall |
| Language, palettes and other local settings | Until you change them, clear app storage or the system removes the relevant data | Adjust available settings in the app or clear app storage |
| Exported files, temporary files and previews | You manage exported files. Temporary or exported copies in app storage may remain until app-data clearing, uninstall or system cleanup | Delete shared exports in your gallery or file manager; clear app storage to remove app-stored copies |
| Feedback email and attachments you send | Only for as long as needed to handle and follow up on feedback, resolve the relevant issue or lawfully address a dispute; deleted when no longer needed, unless retention is legally required | Request deletion through our privacy email. We handle records we hold; email-provider backups are cleaned up under provider rules |
| Agreement-page access data | For as long as needed to deliver pages, maintain necessary functions, protect network security or meet legal duties. Platforms manage their own records under their policies | Email us about records we control; use the relevant platform's channels for its own records |

Deleting artwork in the app does not also delete files you previously exported separately to the gallery or Downloads, or the original images you selected. We cannot remotely erase information that exists only on your device.

The app has no registration or sign-in and therefore no app account to close. You can still delete local data and request deletion of personal information we actually received through feedback email.

## 9. Security and processing locations

The app uses Android app-storage isolation and system permissions to manage local access. Online agreement pages are loaded over HTTPS. We restrict access to feedback information we receive to people who need it.

We do not promise separate application-level encryption for every local artwork file. Device locks, operating-system security, backups and other apps affect data security. No technical measure eliminates every risk.

Local information is primarily stored on your device. Website, email, system-backup or online file services you choose may process information outside your country or region; locations depend on the relevant services. Where processing we arrange requires additional international-transfer safeguards under applicable law, we will meet the relevant notice, consent or other protection requirements.
